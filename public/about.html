About

Built for the frames that get used

Most athlete photography proves attendance. This proves ability. Here is how that difference gets made, and what it means for your session.

The short version

Icflic is a sports photography studio for athletes

Icflic shoots recruiting frames, match-day action and editorial portraits — for the athlete, not the album. Every session starts from the same question: which single image is this actually for, and where is it going to be seen?

That question changes everything downstream. A recruiting frame needs a clean background and a body position a coach can read in half a second. A signing-day post needs to survive being cropped square. A sponsor frame needs vertical headroom for text. Shooting without knowing the destination is how you end up with four hundred photos and nothing you can use.

So the shot list comes first, the session is built around it, and the edit protects it.

How the work gets made

Three things that do not move

Style changes from athlete to athlete. These do not.

01

Light is a decision

Available light is a choice, not a default. If the light on the day will not carry the frame, it gets shaped — with strobes, a scrim, or by moving the whole session forty minutes later.

02

The edit is restrained

Colour stays true to the kit. Skin stays real. No heavy filters that will look dated in a season, and nothing done in the edit that could have been done properly in camera.

03

Delivery is fast

An image that arrives three weeks after the match has already lost most of its value. Previews land inside 24 hours; the full gallery inside five business days.

What you actually get

A gallery, not a hard drive

Every session is delivered as a private online gallery. Full-resolution files for print and recruiting submissions, plus pre-cropped vertical versions so nothing gets mangled by a platform that decides to crop square.

The gallery link can go straight to a coach, a club, or a recruiting service. It stays live for twelve months, and the files are yours to keep under a personal-use licence that covers recruiting, highlight reels and your own social accounts.
