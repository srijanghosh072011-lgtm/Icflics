About Icflic

Photographs that do the talking before you say a word

Most athlete photos are taken from the stands. They prove you were there. They do not show a coach how you move, how you compete, or what you look like when the match is on the line.

Icflic works the other way round. Every session is planned around the frames that actually get used — the one at the top of a recruiting profile, the one a club posts on signing day, the one a sponsor puts on a story. Controlled light, long glass, and edits that hold up at full resolution.

Questions

Everything athletes ask first

How much does a sports photography session cost?

Sessions start at $295 for a one-hour recruiting shoot and run to $850 for full match-day coverage. Every package includes edited high-resolution files, a private online gallery, and social-ready vertical crops. Exact pricing depends on session length, travel and how many final images you need.

See the full package breakdown.

How fast will I get my photos back?

Previews land within 24 hours and the full edited gallery within five business days. Match-day coverage includes a same-night set of ten selects so you have something to post while the result is still fresh. Rush delivery is available when a recruiting or transfer deadline is close.

What should I wear to an athlete photo session?

Bring your match kit, a training kit in a contrasting colour, and one clean off-field outfit. Clean boots photograph far better than new ones. Avoid busy logos that are not yours, and bring any equipment you actually use — real gear looks better than borrowed gear.

Can I use these photos for college recruiting?

Yes. Every package includes a personal-use licence covering recruiting profiles, highlight reels, club and school submissions, and your own social accounts. Coaches and recruiting services can be sent the gallery directly. Commercial and sponsor use is covered by a separate licence, quoted per campaign.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Local sessions carry no travel fee. Anything beyond a 60-mile radius adds a mileage charge quoted before you book, and multi-day tournament or showcase coverage is priced as a flat trip rate.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Overcast light is excellent for portraits, so most sessions go ahead. If conditions are genuinely unworkable, we reschedule at no cost. Match-day coverage runs whenever the match runs — rain and mud tend to make the best frames anyway.
