Privacy policy

Last updated 11 September 2026. This describes exactly what this site collects — nothing more, and no template boilerplate.

The short version

This site sets no cookies for ordinary visitors and runs no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts, styles and images are served from this domain, so loading a page does not tell anyone else that you were here.

The only personal information collected is what you type into the booking or contact form, and it is used solely to answer you and run your session.

What is collected, and when

When you submit a booking request

The booking form collects your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, sport, chosen package, shoot location and any message you add, together with the date and time slot you selected. All of it is needed to confirm and run the session.

When you use the contact form

The contact form collects your name, email address and your message. That is all.

Automatically, for abuse prevention

When a form is submitted, your IP address is combined with a secret value and converted into an irreversible hash before it is stored. The original IP address is never written to the database. The hash is used only to rate-limit submissions and detect spam floods, and it cannot be turned back into your IP address.

Server logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes standard request data, including IP addresses, in order to serve pages and block attacks. That processing is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy and retention periods, which are outside the control of this site.

Cookies

No cookies are set for site visitors, which is why there is no cookie banner. A single session cookie is set for the site owner when they sign in to the private booking panel. It is strictly necessary for authentication, is marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, and expires after eight hours.

How your information is used

  • To reply to your enquiry and confirm or decline a booking request.
  • To plan and deliver the session you booked.
  • To prevent spam and automated abuse of the forms.

Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing. You will not be added to a mailing list by booking.

Who else can see it

Booking and contact submissions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and are readable only by the site owner after signing in. Cloudflare acts as the hosting and infrastructure provider. No other third party receives your information.

How long it is kept

  • Booking requests: for 24 months after the session date, for scheduling and accounting.
  • Contact messages: for 12 months.
  • Hashed IP records: rolling rate-limit records are discarded within 24 hours.
  • Delivered galleries: 12 months from delivery, as described in the terms.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email hello@icflic.com and it will be actioned within 30 days. Deleting a booking record before the session necessarily cancels the booking.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or the CCPA. Those rights are honoured regardless of where you are.

Children

Sessions are frequently booked by athletes under 18. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must submit the booking and countersign the session agreement. No information is knowingly collected from a child under 13. If you believe that has happened, email hello@icflic.com and the record will be deleted.

Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. The site enforces a strict Content Security Policy, and the private booking panel is protected by a hashed password, rate limiting, and CSRF tokens. No payment card details are ever handled by this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes affecting existing bookings will be emailed to affected clients.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@icflic.com.
