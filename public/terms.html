Terms of service

Last updated 11 September 2026. These terms apply to every session booked through this site.

1. Booking and confirmation

Submitting the booking form creates a request, not a confirmed session. A slot is only held once you receive a written confirmation by email. Requests are normally answered within 48 hours.

2. Deposits and payment

A deposit of 25% of the session fee is due within 72 hours of confirmation and holds the date. The remaining balance is due on delivery of the gallery. Prices are quoted in US dollars and exclude sales tax where it applies.

Payment is handled outside this website. This site never sees, stores or processes card details.

3. Cancellation and rescheduling

  • Cancel or reschedule more than 72 hours before the session: the deposit is refunded in full, or moved to a new date.
  • Cancel or reschedule within 72 hours: the deposit may be moved once to a new date, after which it is forfeited.
  • Cancellation by the photographer for any reason: the deposit is refunded in full.
  • Weather cancellations never count against your one reschedule.

4. Weather and conditions

Overcast conditions are workable and sessions generally proceed. Where conditions are unsafe or genuinely unworkable, the session is rescheduled at no cost. Match-day coverage proceeds whenever the fixture proceeds.

5. Delivery

Preview images are delivered within 24 hours of the session. The full edited gallery is delivered within five business days, or within 48 hours where the rush add-on has been purchased. Galleries remain available for 12 months from delivery.

The number of edited images stated in each package is a minimum, not a cap. Unedited or raw files are not delivered.

Copyright in all images remains with Icflic. You receive a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive personal-use licence which permits:

The personal-use licence does not permit resale, licensing to third parties, use in paid advertising, or use by a brand, sponsor, club or media outlet. Those uses require a separate commercial licence, quoted per campaign. The personal-brand package includes an extended licence covering sponsor posts on your own channels.

Images may not be re-edited, re-cropped beyond the supplied crops, filtered, or have the colour altered before publication. Credit is appreciated but not required for personal use.

7. Portfolio use

Icflic may use images from your session in its portfolio, on this website and on its own social accounts, unless you ask in writing for your session to be kept private. That request is honoured permanently and at no cost — just say so when you book.

8. Minors

Where the athlete is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must submit the booking and agrees to these terms on the athlete's behalf. A guardian must be reachable for the duration of the session.

9. Liability

Icflic carries its own equipment insurance. Liability for any claim arising from a session is limited to the total fee paid for that session. Icflic is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost recruiting or sponsorship opportunities.

In the event of equipment failure, illness or another circumstance preventing delivery, liability is limited to a full refund of all amounts paid.

10. Conduct

Sessions take place in public and private sports facilities. You are responsible for securing any permission needed to shoot at your chosen location. Sessions may be ended without refund where conduct is unsafe or abusive.

11. Changes to these terms

These terms may change. The version in force for your session is the version published on the date your booking was confirmed.
