Questions

Straight answers

Pricing, turnaround, usage rights, travel and what happens when it rains. If something is not covered here, ask directly and it will get added.

Section 01

Pricing and booking

How much does a sports photography session cost?

Sessions start at $295 for a one-hour recruiting shoot, $450 for a lit editorial portrait session, and $850 for full match-day coverage. Monthly personal-brand content runs $650 per month. Every package includes edited high-resolution files, a private online gallery, and social-ready vertical crops.

Full package breakdown.

How do I book a session?

Use the booking page. It shows genuine availability — dates that are greyed out are actually taken or blocked off. Pick a date and time, send the request, and you will get a confirmation by email once it is accepted. Booking takes about two minutes and does not require an account.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

Yes. A 25% deposit holds the date and is due once the request is confirmed. The balance is due on delivery of the gallery. The deposit is refundable up to 72 hours before the session.

What if I need to reschedule?

Reschedule free of charge up to 72 hours before the session. Inside 72 hours the deposit moves to the new date once, after which it is forfeited. Weather cancellations never count against you.

Section 02

The session itself

What should I wear to an athlete photo session?

Bring your match kit, a training kit in a contrasting colour, and one clean off-field outfit. Clean boots photograph far better than new ones. Avoid busy logos that are not yours, and bring any equipment you actually use — real gear photographs better than borrowed gear.

How long does a session take?

A recruiting session is one hour on location. An editorial portrait session is 90 minutes including the lighting setup. Match-day coverage runs from warm-up to the final whistle, so plan for roughly three hours depending on the sport.

Where do sessions happen?

Wherever the frames are best. Your home pitch or court, a training ground, a stadium, or a studio for lit portraits. If you do not have a location in mind, one gets suggested in the brief you receive before the session.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Overcast light is excellent for portraits, so most sessions go ahead. If conditions are genuinely unworkable, we reschedule at no cost. Match-day coverage runs whenever the match runs — rain and mud tend to make the best frames anyway.

Can my parents or coach come?

Yes, and it often helps. One extra person who knows your sport is useful for spotting technique that reads badly on camera. More than two tends to slow the session down.

Section 03

Delivery and usage

How fast will I get my photos back?

Previews land within 24 hours and the full edited gallery within five business days. Match-day coverage includes a same-night set of ten selects so you have something to post while the result is still fresh. Rush delivery inside 48 hours is available as a $95 add-on.

Can I use these photos for college recruiting?

Yes. Every package includes a personal-use licence covering recruiting profiles, highlight reels, club and school submissions, and your own social accounts. Coaches and recruiting services can be sent the gallery link directly.

Can a sponsor or brand use my photos?

Not under the standard licence. Paid placements, kit launches and sponsor campaigns need a commercial licence, quoted per campaign. The personal-brand package includes an extended licence that already covers sponsor posts on your own channels.

Do I get the unedited files?

No. The edit is part of the work, and unedited frames do not represent it. If you need more selects than the package includes, extra edited files can be added from the same session at any time.

How long do you keep my gallery online?

Twelve months from delivery. Download everything you want to keep before then. Archives can be restored after that window for a small retrieval fee, but it is not guaranteed indefinitely.

Section 04

Travel and logistics

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Local sessions carry no travel fee. Anything beyond a 60-mile radius adds a mileage charge quoted before you book, and multi-day tournament or showcase coverage is priced as a flat trip rate.

Do you cover tournaments and showcases?

Yes. Multi-day events are quoted as a flat trip rate covering travel, all match days, and delivery. Book these as early as possible — showcase weekends fill first.

What sports do you shoot?

Soccer most often, plus track and field, basketball and football. Other sports are almost always a yes — send the details and you will get a straight answer about whether it is a good fit.
